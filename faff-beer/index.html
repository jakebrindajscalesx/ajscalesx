Join the fight

Beer's biggest enemy isn't a bad brewer. It's time.

Hoppy beer is brutally perishable — it can lose most of what made it great within weeks of packaging. After one too many overpriced, badly handled four-packs, we drew a line in the sand. There's now another option. It's F.A.F.F.

We rebuilt brewing and distribution around a single obsession: freshness before everything else. Here's how it plays out, every single release.

  1. 01

    Small, limited batch

    We brew a genuinely small batch of hop-forward beer — always an IPA, always uncompromising.

  2. 02

    Pre-sale opens

    When the batch is nearly ready, pre-sale opens to the mailing list. You've got about an hour to lock in your allocation.

  3. 03

    Canned Monday, posted Monday

    Once it's ready, we can the batch on a Monday and get it in the post that same day.

  4. 04

    In your hands, days old

    You crack a beer that's days old — not the nine-month-old four-pack sitting on a bottle-shop shelf for $35.

This is not a subscription. It's first in, best dressed, on release day. Sign up to the mailing list to join the fight against expensive, old beer.

About us (me)

Lachy Crothers. Career brewer. Beer judge. Lover of sh!t hot piss.

F.A.F.F is 100% owned and operated by Lachy Crothers — brewing consultant and self-proclaimed lover of properly fresh beer. It's the passion project that brings his obsession with fresh, hoppy beer to life.

The best part of Lachy's career has always been drinking amazing beer straight from the tank. F.A.F.F exists so more people get to experience that same joy — the way it's meant to be, before time gets to it.
