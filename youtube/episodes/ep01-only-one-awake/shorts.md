## For every Short

- **On-screen text, first 2 seconds** (use different words from the spoken line):
  1. "72 HOURS LEFT" · 2. "ONLY YOU. AND THE DOGS." · 3. "DON'T ANSWER IT" · 4. "WAIT… WHAT?" · 5. "NOBODY FLYING"
- **Loop point:** end each one on a question the first line answers. For example, Short 3 ends on "*This
  number is not in service*", which loops straight back into "Every phone on Earth is ringing…".
- **Vertical framing:** the animation is 16:9. Centre-crop on the character. The plane beat (Short
  5) needs the sky and the plane in frame, so re-frame it vertically instead of just cropping it.
- **Pinned comment:** "Full story: [link to long video]".
