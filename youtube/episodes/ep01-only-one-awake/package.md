# Packaging: title and thumbnail

All titles were linted with `yt-package/title.py`. All 10 follow the reference channel's
"What IF You…" pattern.

## Winner

**Title:** What IF You Were the Only One Awake for 72 Hours?
**Thumbnail text:** DON'T WAKE THEM
**Pairing score:** 100/100. 49 characters, has a number, and the thumbnail and title use different words.

Why this pair: the title promises a survival countdown (72 hours). The thumbnail adds a warning
the title doesn't give. "Don't wake them" is the opposite of what the viewer expects you to want,
and the twist pays it off.

## Thumbnail brief

- **Character:** the "you" character, centre-left, eyes wide, finger to lips (*shh*). The
  expression is fear, not surprise.
- **Background:** a dark city at night with every window lit but no people. Behind the character, one
  passenger jet flies dead level across the sky. Desaturated blue background with a warm orange
  rim light on the character, so it holds contrast at feed size.
- **Text:** **DON'T WAKE THEM**, three words, top-right, heavy white font with a black stroke.
  The word "DON'T" is in red.
- **Small detail for repeat viewers:** a digital clock in the corner reading **3:12**.
- **Test at feed size:** shrink it to 168×94 px. If "DON'T WAKE THEM" can't be read, make it bigger. If
  the plane disappears, drop it. The face and text are what matter.
- **Match the reference channel's look:** bold flat-shaded animated character, high saturation,
  one big emotion.
