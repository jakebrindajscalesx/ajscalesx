# AJScalesX — PA Check-In System

## HOW TO USE ME AS YOUR PA

Just say any of these to me at any time:

---

### "Check for replies"
I'll scan your Gmail inbox for replies from prospects, update the dashboard and CSV, and tell you:
- Who replied and what they said
- What to say back (I'll write the reply for you)
- Whether they're hot, warm, or cold

---

### "Update my dashboard"
I'll update `dashboard.html` with the latest stats, move prospects through the pipeline, and push to the repo.

---

### "Add a prospect: [name, email, niche, city]"
I'll add them to `prospects.csv` and queue an email draft in Gmail for you.

---

### "Send follow-ups"
I'll check who hasn't replied after 3 days and queue follow-up draft emails in Gmail for all of them.

---

### "Write a reply to [name]"
Tell me what they said and I'll write a perfect reply for you to send.

---

### "How are we tracking?"
I'll give you a full summary: emails sent, replies received, pipeline status, revenue to date, what to do next.

---

### "I closed [name] for $[amount]"
I'll update the dashboard, mark them as Closed Won, update revenue, and prep the website brief template for you.

---
