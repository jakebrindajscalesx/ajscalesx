# How to Find Prospects: QLD Businesses Without Websites
## AJScalesX Outreach — 7-Day Sprint Guide

---

## FASTEST METHOD: Google Maps (30 mins = 20+ prospects)

1. Open **Google Maps** on your phone or computer
2. Search: `painters brisbane` (or any trade + suburb)
3. Click through each listing
4. **Look for the red flag:** No website button = GOLD PROSPECT
5. Tap/click their name → copy their phone number
6. **Call or text them** (calls convert 3x better than email for tradies)
7. Repeat for: plumbers, electricians, landscapers, cleaners, gardeners, carpenters

**Best QLD suburbs to search:**
- Brisbane: Springwood, Capalaba, Redlands, Logan, Ipswich, Caboolture
- Gold Coast: Nerang, Coomera, Pimpama, Helensvale, Robina
- Sunshine Coast: Nambour, Caloundra, Maroochydore, Noosa Heads

**Google Maps search strings (copy-paste ready):**
```
painters springwood brisbane

electricians logan qld

carpenters ipswich qld
gardeners helensvale gold coast
handyman caboolture qld
```

## METHOD 2: Facebook Business Pages (20 mins = 10+ prospects)

1. Open Facebook
2. Search: `Brisbane painter` or `Gold Coast landscaper`
3. Click **Pages** tab
4. Look for pages with:
   - No website linked in their "About" section
   - Last post more than 1 month ago (they need help)
   - Under 200 followers (small local business)
5. Send them a Facebook DM using Template 1 (personalised to their niche)

**Facebook DM version (shorter, more casual):**
> "Hey [Name], noticed [Business Name] doesn't have a website yet. I build 5-page websites for QLD tradies for $500 — usually done in 5 days. Want me to send a free mock-up of what yours could look like? — AJ from AJScalesX"

---

## METHOD 3: Yellow Pages / True Local Scrape

1. Go to **yellowpages.com.au**
2. Search for a trade + suburb (e.g., "painters brisbane southside")
3. Look for listings with **no website link** shown
4. Copy their phone number or email
5. Contact via phone/email using the templates

---

## METHOD 4: Airtasker Profile Outreach

1. Go to **airtasker.com.au** and browse local service providers in QLD
2. Many tradies on Airtasker have no website (they rely on the platform)
3. Find their profile, note their name/trade
4. Google "[Name] + [Trade] + [Suburb]" to find their phone number
5. Call or email them

---

## METHOD 5: Gumtree Ads

1. Go to **gumtree.com.au**
2. Search: Handyman / Cleaner / Painter in Brisbane Region or Gold Coast
3. Small businesses advertising here often have NO website
4. Email/call directly from the ad

## DAILY TRACKING

Use `prospects.csv` to track every lead. Update status to:
- `Prospect` — not yet contacted
- `Emailed` — first email sent
- `Followed Up` — follow-up sent
- `Replied` — they responded (positive or negative)
- `Interested` — they want to proceed
- `Closed Won` — paid, building website
- `Closed Lost` — not interested
