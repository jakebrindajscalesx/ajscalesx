## CLOSING THE DEAL: SCRIPTS

### When they reply with interest:

> "Great to hear from you! I'd love to put together a quick mock-up of your homepage so you can see exactly what it'll look like. Could you send me your logo (or your business name/colours if you don't have one), and a few sentences about what you do? I'll have it back to you within 24 hours."

### When they ask for price:

> "A 5-page starter website is $500 AUD all up — no hidden fees. That covers design, build, mobile optimisation, and launch. I take 50% upfront ($250) to start, and the rest when it's live. Would you like to go ahead?"

### When they say "too expensive":

> "I totally get it — budget is always a factor. Can I ask, what were you expecting to invest? I might be able to work something out. Alternatively, I can do a 3-page version for $350 if that helps. The important thing is getting you online and getting enquiries coming in."
