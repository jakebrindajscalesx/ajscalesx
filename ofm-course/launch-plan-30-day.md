# 30-Day Launch Plan — OFM Agency Course

**Business:** Krown Management — $97 one-time course
**Channel:** Instagram (organic) → Skool checkout
**Goal:** Course live, first 20 sales ($1,940) by day 30

---

## Week 1 — Build
- [ ] Register business entity / bank account (legal-compliance-checklist.md, section 1)
- [ ] Sign the profit-split agreement with your mate
- [ ] Set up Skool community (skool-setup-guide.md)
- [ ] Paste course-content.md into Skool modules
- [ ] Generate course-content.pdf as the downloadable workbook
- [ ] Build/adapt sales-page.html or Skool community page copy
- [ ] Film first 5 Instagram videos (pick hooks 1-5 from instagram-promo-playbook.md)

## Week 2 — Warm-up content
- [ ] Post 5-6 videos (pillars: proof, behind-the-scenes, education — no direct offer yet)
- [ ] Engage with comments/DMs, build initial audience trust
- [ ] Soft-mention "course coming soon" in 1-2 posts
- [ ] Finish filming/recording any video lessons for Skool if adding video to modules

## Week 3 — Launch week
- [ ] Announce course launch (post daily, mix education + direct offer)
- [ ] Post hook #20 ("everything in my course in 60 seconds") as the main CTA video
- [ ] Enable "founding price" $97 (vs $197 regular) — mention it's limited-time honestly (e.g., first 20 buyers, then it does return to $197 — must be true if stated)
- [ ] Respond personally to every DM asking about the course
- [ ] Track: link clicks → Skool visits → purchases

## Week 4 — Push + collect proof
- [ ] Keep posting daily, lean into case studies from Week 3 buyers (with permission)
- [ ] Send a check-in message to buyers asking how Module 1-2 is landing (early feedback = testimonials)
- [ ] Review numbers: total sales, refund rate, best-performing video/hook
- [ ] Plan month 2: double down on whatever hook/pillar converted best

---

## Metrics to track weekly

| Metric | Target by Day 30 |
|---|---|
| Instagram followers gained | 500+ |
| Bio link clicks | 300+ |
| Skool community joins (free) | 100+ |
| Paid conversions | 20+ |
| Revenue | $1,940+ |
| Refund rate | Under 10% |

## After day 30

- Raise price back toward $197 once founding-cohort sells out (per business-plan.md pricing rationale).
- Start testing the upsell path: a $500-1,000 "done-with-you" offer for buyers who want direct help signing their first creator (only once you have real course buyers to test demand with — don't build this before there's demand signal).
