# Skool Setup Guide

Step-by-step to get the course live on Skool, matching the business plan (low-ticket, one-time $97 purchase, sold via Instagram bio link).

## 1. Create the community
- Name: something tied to the brand your mate is building on Instagram (e.g., "[Mate's Name] OFM Academy") — keep it business/education-sounding, not adult-adjacent, since Skool's discovery surfaces community names publicly.
- Description: 2-3 lines, business-education framing (see sales-page copy below).
- Set community to **Paid** with a **one-time payment** option (Skool supports one-time in addition to monthly) — price at $97, with a "regular price $197" strikethrough if Skool's plan builder supports it, otherwise state it in the sales copy.

## 2. Course structure inside Skool
Create a "Classroom" with 8 sections matching `course-content.md`:
1. Understanding the OFM Business Model
2. Finding & Signing Your First Creator
3. Content Strategy & Growth Marketing
4. Chatting & Monetization
5. Contracts, Pricing & Getting Paid
6. Tools & Systems
7. Scaling: Hiring a Team
8. Longevity: Legal, Reputation & Staying in Business

Paste each module's lessons as separate Skool lessons within that section. Attach the PDF workbook (`course-content.pdf`) as a downloadable resource on the first lesson, and the contract template as a resource on Module 5.

## 3. Free preview
Make Module 1 (or just Lesson 1.1) free/public preview — this is what converts Instagram traffic. It should hook them enough to buy without giving away the actionable "how."

## 4. Community feed
Post a welcome pinned post with:
- What the course covers
- How to get support (comment on lessons, or a weekly live Q&A if your mate wants to run one)
- The earnings disclaimer (paste from legal-compliance-checklist.md)

## 5. Payment & refund settings
- Connect Stripe (Skool handles this natively).
- Set refund policy in the community's terms/about section: 7-day no-questions refund.
- Keep product description in Skool as "business/educational course on agency management" — don't reference adult content in the payment description (this matters for Stripe's risk review).

## 6. Link in bio
Use Skool's community URL directly, or a simple Linktree/bio-link page pointing to it — either works. Keep the destination URL name business-neutral in case Instagram reviews link destinations for ad-policy compliance.

## 7. Launch checklist
- [ ] Community created, priced, one-time $97 payment configured
- [ ] 8 modules built, lessons pasted in from course-content.md
- [ ] PDF workbook + contract template attached as resources
- [ ] Free preview lesson set
- [ ] Refund policy + earnings disclaimer posted
- [ ] Stripe connected and tested with a $1 test transaction
- [ ] Bio link live on Instagram
