# Instagram Promo Playbook — For Your Mate (The Face)

Goal: post consistent, ad-policy-safe, credibility-building content that funnels to the Skool link in bio. None of this shows or references explicit content — it's framed exactly like any "how I built my agency" business creator content.

**Ad-policy safety rule of thumb:** if the post would be fine coming from someone teaching "how to start a social media marketing agency," it's safe. If it references, implies, or shows adult content, cut it. See `legal-compliance-checklist.md` section 3.

---

## Content pillars (rotate through these)

1. **Proof/results** — dashboard screenshots (blurred/redacted sensitive info), revenue milestones, "another account crossed $X this month."
2. **Behind-the-scenes ops** — a day in the life managing accounts, what a chat shift looks like (described, not shown), the tools/systems used.
3. **Educational hooks** — one tactical tip per video pulled straight from the course modules (growth funnel, PPV timing, contract basics).
4. **Myth-busting / objection handling** — "No, you don't need to be a creator to make money in this space," "Here's what actually makes an agency fail."
5. **Direct offer** — course launch, price, what's included, link in bio.

Ratio: roughly 60% education/proof, 30% behind-the-scenes, 10% direct offer.

## Caption/CTA formula

```
[Hook line matching the video]

[2-3 lines expanding the point / a specific tactic or number]

Want the full breakdown — contracts, pricing, systems, how to sign your first
creator? Full course is linked in bio. $97 founding price, [X] spots/days left.
```

Keep captions short. The video does the work; the caption reinforces + links.

## Posting cadence (first 30 days)

| Week | Cadence | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4-5 posts | Pillars 1-3, build initial credibility, no hard offer yet |
| 2 | 5-6 posts | Add pillar 4 (myth-busting), soft mention of course coming |
| 3 | 6-7 posts | Course launch week — mix all pillars, 2-3 direct offer posts |
| 4 | 5-6 posts | Case studies from first buyers, keep offer posts going |

## What NOT to post (ad-policy / platform-risk list)

- Any adult/explicit imagery, even blurred or "artistic"
- Screenshots that reveal a creator's identity/handle without their permission
- Messages/DMs that reference explicit content
- Guaranteed income claims ("you'll make $10k in 30 days")
- Anything implying you help people bypass age/ID verification
