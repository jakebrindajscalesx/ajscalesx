## THE NICHE (specific, not generic)

"Productivity tips" alone is oversaturated. The angle we're running:

> **Digital systems for overwhelmed professionals** — short videos showing exactly how to set up an app, shortcut, or workflow that saves time, framed as "steal my system."

This works because:
- **Evergreen** — people are always behind on email, tasks, and organization. No trend dependency.
- **Clear monetization hook** — every video naturally points at an app (Notion, Todoist, Google Calendar), a physical product (desk organizer, planner, monitor stand), or a digital template we can sell later.
- **Low production bar** — screen recordings + voiceover + text overlays. No face, no location, no expensive gear. CapCut (free) covers 90% of it.

**Working handle concept:** something like `@systemstacked`, `@dailysystemhacks`, or `@lazyproductivity` — final handle to be picked at account-creation step (see `setup_checklist.md`), since availability needs checking live.

## MONETIZATION ROADMAP (realistic, not hype)

Growing an Instagram account from zero is slower than local outreach — there's no benchmark that gets you $1,000 in week one. Setting honest milestones:

| Phase | Timeframe | Goal | Primary metric |
|-------|-----------|------|-----------------|
| **Launch** | Days 1–7 | Account live, 5 videos posted, link-in-bio + lead magnet built | Consistency, not views |
| **Traction** | Days 8–30 | Daily posting, 3+ affiliate programs approved, first email subscribers | Follower growth, saves/shares |
| **First revenue** | Days 30–60 | First affiliate clicks convert to commission, 500+ email list | First $ earned |
| **Own product** | Day 60–90 | Launch $19–$27 digital template using audience feedback from Phase 1–2 | Product revenue > affiliate revenue |

Affiliate commissions on early-stage accounts (sub-5k followers) are typically small ($0–$50/week) until watch time and trust compound. The real asset being built in the first 30 days is the **content library + email list**, not immediate cash.

## WHAT AUTOMATION ACTUALLY MEANS HERE

To be direct about scope: I (Claude Code) can build and run the **content and scheduling pipeline** — script generation, caption/hashtag writing, content calendar tracking, a dashboard, batch-producing scripts for you to record or feed into a text-to-video tool.

I can't and won't:
- Create the Instagram account itself (needs your phone/email + you accepting Meta's terms)
- Run engagement automation (auto-liking, follow/unfollow bots, fake engagement) — this violates Instagram's Terms of Service and risks the account being shadowbanned or banned outright
- Post on your behalf without you granting a Meta Graph API token tied to your own Business account

Legitimate automation = **scheduled publishing of real content you approve**, via Meta's official Graph API or a scheduler like Meta Business Suite/Later — not engagement manipulation. That's the model this plan builds toward.
