# Setup Checklist — Things Only You Can Do

I can write scripts, captions, and manage the tracking dashboard, but the steps below need a human with a phone, an email, and legal authority to accept Meta's terms. Do these in order — nothing else in this folder is useful until Step 1–3 are done.

---

## STEP 1 — Create the Instagram account
- [x] Pick a handle — **@steal_mysystem**
- [ ] Sign up with a dedicated email (not your personal one) — recommend a free Gmail/Outlook just for this business
- [ ] Write a bio: one line on what the account does + "link below" pointing at your future link-in-bio page
- [ ] Add a profile photo/logo (Canva → simple wordmark is fine, no face needed)

## STEP 2 — Switch to a Professional account
- [x] Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to Professional Account
- [x] Chose **Business** (unlocks Meta Business Suite, branded-content tagging, and API access later)
- [x] Category: "Digital creator"

## STEP 3 — Link-in-bio + email capture
- [ ] Create a free Beacons.ai or Linktree page
- [ ] Add a placeholder lead magnet link (we'll build the actual PDF/template together once you're set up — tell me "build the lead magnet" and I'll draft it)
- [ ] Sign up for Beehiiv or ConvertKit free tier, connect the signup form to your link-in-bio page

## STEP 4 — Affiliate program applications
- [ ] Amazon Associates — sign up at Amazon's official affiliate program page (search "Amazon Associates" from your Amazon account, it's under Amazon's own site — don't use a third-party link)
- [ ] Apply to the app-based programs listed in `affiliate_programs.md` once you have at least a bio + 3 posted videos (most programs want to see the account is real before approving)
- [ ] Come back and tell me which ones got approved — I'll update `dashboard.html` and start weaving those specific products into scripts

## STEP 5 — (Later, once posting consistently) Meta Graph API for scheduling
Only needed once you want to batch-schedule instead of posting manually each day.
- [ ] Create a Facebook Page and link it to your Instagram Business account (required — IG API access is routed through a linked FB Page)
- [ ] Go to developers.facebook.com, create a Meta app, add the "Instagram Graph API" product
- [ ] Generate a long-lived access token for your own account (never share this token — treat it like a password)
- [ ] Tell me you have the token + your Instagram Business Account ID and I'll write the publishing script that posts pre-approved content on schedule

**Do not skip to Step 5 first.** Manual posting for the first 2–3 weeks is fine and lower-risk — it lets you learn what content actually performs before automating publishing.

---

## GUARDRAILS — what this automation will and won't do

✅ Will do: generate scripts/captions/hashtags, track a content calendar, help draft the lead magnet, eventually schedule *your own approved* posts via the official API.

🚫 Won't do: auto-follow/unfollow, auto-like, buy followers, run engagement bots, or anything that mimics human interaction to inflate metrics. These violate Instagram's Terms of Service and are the #1 cause of otherwise-legitimate accounts getting shadowbanned or disabled. Growth here comes from consistent, real content — not manipulation.
