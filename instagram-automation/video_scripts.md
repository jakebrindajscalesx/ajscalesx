# Video Scripts — Batch 1 (Days 1–15)

Format: faceless, screen recording or stock/AI B-roll + voiceover (CapCut auto-voice or your recorded voice) + on-screen text overlay for every key line. Each script is 20–40 seconds — Reels reward short, fast-cut videos in this niche.

**How to shoot these:** either (a) screen-record yourself actually using the app on your phone/laptop while reading the script as voiceover, or (b) feed the script into an AI video tool (CapCut's text-to-speech + stock footage, or Canva) if you don't want to record your own screen yet.

Legend: 🎯 = affiliate/product tie-in for this video.

---

### 1. "The 2-minute rule that killed my to-do list anxiety"
- **Hook (0–3s):** "If a task takes less than 2 minutes, you're not allowed to write it on your to-do list."
- **Body:** Explain the 2-minute rule — do it now instead of logging it. Show a messy to-do app, then show tasks disappearing as you "just do them."
- **On-screen text:** "2 MIN RULE" → "Do it. Don't log it."
- **CTA:** "Save this before your list gets out of control again."
- 🎯 None (pure value — builds trust for later videos)
