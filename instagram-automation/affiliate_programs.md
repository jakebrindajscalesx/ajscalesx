# Affiliate Programs to Apply To

Apply directly through each company's own official site/affiliate portal — search "[program name] affiliate program" and go to the company's own domain (avoid random third-party "get affiliate links here" sites, some are scams). Don't click links from search ads; go to the organic/official result.

Most programs want to see a real, active account before approving — apply after you have a bio + 3–5 posted videos, not before.

| Program | Why it fits this niche | Typical model | Priority |
|---------|------------------------|----------------|----------|
| **Amazon Associates** | Desk items, planners, cable organizers, notebooks — near-infinite product tie-ins | Small % commission per sale, short cookie window | Apply Day 1 (easiest approval, no follower minimum) |
| **Notion affiliate/creator program** | Central to several scripts (Batch 1 #2, #9, #13) | Commission or credit per paid signup | Apply once 3+ Notion-themed videos are posted |
| **Skillshare affiliate program** | Natural fit for "learn a skill / build a system" CTAs; pairs well with productivity content | Flat fee per free trial signup | Apply Week 2 |
| **Todoist / Sunsama / ClickUp** (pick 1, don't spread thin) | App-demo videos (#6, #9) | Commission per paid conversion | Apply once you've picked which app you'll actually demo on camera |
| **Audible/Amazon** | Ties to script #11 (reading habit content) | Flat fee per signup | Apply Day 1 alongside Amazon Associates |

## APPLICATION SEQUENCE
1. **Day 1:** Amazon Associates (and Audible if bundled) — no follower minimum, fastest approval
2. **Day 7 (after first week of posts):** Whichever app-affiliate program matches your best-performing video from Batch 1
3. **Day 14:** Skillshare or a second app program, once there's a consistent posting track record

## RULES FOR USING AFFILIATE LINKS
- Always disclose — add "#ad" or "affiliate link" in the caption when a video pushes a specific paid product. This is a legal requirement (FTC-style disclosure), not optional.
- Never recommend a product in a script you haven't actually verified does what the script claims — approval aside, false claims kill trust and can violate the affiliate program's own terms.
