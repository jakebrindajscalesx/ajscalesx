# AJScalesX — Instagram PA Check-In System

## HOW TO USE ME AS YOUR PA

Just say any of these to me at any time:

---

### "Log today's post"
Tell me the video title/script number, and I'll mark it Posted in `content_calendar.csv` and update the dashboard.

---

### "Update my dashboard"
Give me current follower count, views/saves on recent posts, and email list size — I'll update `dashboard.html` and push to the repo.

---

### "Write hashtag sets"
I'll generate 3 rotating hashtag sets (broad/medium/niche mix) tailored to whichever script you're posting next.

---

### "Build the lead magnet"
I'll write the actual content for the free PDF/Notion template promised in the scripts (e.g. "5 systems" or "closing checklist" template).

---

### "Write Batch 2 scripts"
Tell me which Batch 1 videos performed best (views/saves/comments) and I'll write the next 15 scripts skewed toward that format/topic.

---

### "An affiliate program got approved: [name]"
I'll update `affiliate_programs.md` and `dashboard.html`, and tag the matching scripts/calendar rows with real links.

---

### "I got a sale/commission: [program, amount]"
I'll update revenue tracking on the dashboard and log it.

---

### "How are we tracking?"
Full summary: posts published, follower growth, email list size, affiliate status, revenue to date, what to do next.

---

## WEEKLY CHECK-IN ROUTINE (recommended)

**Daily (2 mins):** "Log today's post" — after each video goes up.

**Weekly (10 mins, e.g. Sunday):** "Update my dashboard — here's this week's numbers" → then "how are we tracking?"
